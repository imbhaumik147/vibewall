Simple Workflow

How to Create Your Wallpaper

Follow 4 straightforward steps to generate a personalized aesthetic collage wallpaper.

1

Upload Any Photos

Drag & drop your favorite photos, anime art, moodboard aesthetics, or album covers. No limits on image count.

2

Pick Layout & Heroes

Choose Pinterest mosaic or custom grid. Click the gold Star on any photo in the table to feature it as a larger Hero.

3

Customize Sizing & Tone

Scale hero tiles (2×2 to 5×5), toggle vertical & horizontal spans, adjust gap spacing, background color, and photo tone filters.

4

Export in Lossless HD

Export full-resolution PNG or JPG wallpapers optimized precisely for iPhone, Samsung, MacBook, and iPad screens.
